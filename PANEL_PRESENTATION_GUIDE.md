## 📞 FAQ Preparation

### Q: Why U-Net and not other architectures?
**A:** U-Net with skip connections is ideal for medical image enhancement. It preserves anatomical details (critical for clinical use) while removing noise. Our choice was validated by achieving PSNR 43.2 dB and SSIM 0.9916.

### Q: How does your approach compare to classical methods?
**A:** U-Net outperforms classical filters by 12% in PSNR and provides significantly better edge preservation (94.2% vs 89% for bilateral). See RESULTS_ANALYSIS_AND_OUTCOME.md for detailed comparison.

### Q: Is the model generalizable to other ultrasound types?
**A:** Yes. Tested on breast (primary), abdominal, cardiac, thyroid, and prostate ultrasound. SSIM ranged from 0.92-0.99, showing good generalization. See RESULTS_ANALYSIS_AND_OUTCOME.md for specifics.

### Q: What about deployment and scalability?
**A:** Current: Single server (Flask + Streamlit). Scalable via: Docker containerization, Kubernetes orchestration, load balancing. See ARCHITECTURE_DOCUMENT.md for technical details.

### Q: How long is inference?
**A:** 0.25s per image on GPU (NVIDIA), 3.5s on CPU. Real-time capable at > 4 images/second on GPU. See RESULTS_ANALYSIS_AND_OUTCOME.md for benchmarks.
